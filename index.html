Questions

Good to know

How long does a photoshoot last?

Every session lasts 45 minutes. It is enough time to relax, walk the route and get a varied gallery without getting tired.

How many people can join?

The solo session is for 1 person, the couples session for 2, and the family session for up to 4 people.

When do I receive my photos?

Solo and couples galleries are delivered in 1–2 business days. Family galleries take 2–3 business days. You receive a private online gallery to view and download.

Where does the photoshoot take place?

You choose a route when you book: Old Town (Mercado Central, Plaza del Patriarca, Plaza de la Reina, Cathedral), City of Arts and Sciences, or El Saler Beach (couples and families).

Do I need to know how to pose?

No. I guide you with simple prompts and movement so everything feels natural, even if you hate being photographed.
